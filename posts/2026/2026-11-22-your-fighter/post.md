## Mastodon

Fighters is step 4 of 5 of New warband. Beside each fighter, one button recruits another of the same, with the same wargear at its full price, and one removes it. The new one waits for a name of its own. Fantasy City Warbands is a free app for your warband in Black Death City, Necropolis and Wyrdcry.

One small post a day on building a warband.
