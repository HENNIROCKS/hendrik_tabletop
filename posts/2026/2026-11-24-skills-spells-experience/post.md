## Mastodon

Fighters is step 4 of 5 of New warband. In Black Death City some fighters pick skills or a spell when recruited, and each starts with experience such as 20+d6: roll at the table, tap the result. Then wargear. Fantasy City Warbands is a free app for your warband in Black Death City, Necropolis and Wyrdcry.

One post a day on building a warband.
