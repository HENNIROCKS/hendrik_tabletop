## Mastodon

Choice is step 3 of 5 of New warband: what the whole warband picks, where its game asks for it. In Black Death City an origin, in Wyrdcry the Mercenaries' State Pride; elsewhere there is nothing to choose. Fantasy City Warbands is a free app for your warband in Black Death City, Necropolis and Wyrdcry.

One small post a day on building a warband.
